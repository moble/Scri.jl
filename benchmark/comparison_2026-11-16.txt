A time marked ~ was not measured.  For a gradient, it is the number of
evaluations times the time of one, because the gradient would have taken
longer than the limit of 60 s.
For Python `scri`, no gradient is ever run, and an evaluation marked ~ was
timed with fewer time samples and scaled up.  It transforms the shear and
all five Weyl components for either of the sets that has Weyl components,
so that transformation was timed once, for the set with three of them, and
the set with all five repeats those times.
